Help – LH Audio Editor

  1. ☰ › Open audio file… › pick the lesson recording (Dropbox, Downloads…). It appears on the Recording track.
  2. ▶ Play, listen, and press + Marker (or M) at points you want to remember, even while it plays.
  3. Type a note for each marker in the Markers list below. Tap a marker's time to go there.
  4. With Select, drag across the recording to choose a section. Double-tap between two markers to select exactly that part. Drag the selection's edges to adjust it.
  5. Move to Excerpts lifts the section onto the second track (it leaves a gap in the recording). Copy to Excerpts keeps the recording whole.
  6. Type a name for the excerpt in the bar under the tracks, or in the Excerpts list.
  7. Share › Notes puts the excerpt into Apple Notes as an audio file named after it. Share all sends every excerpt at once. Markers › Share as text sends your marker notes.

Moving around: pinch to zoom, two fingers to scroll, or drag along the strip at the top (the whole recording). With Move, drag a clip to move it (also to the other track), drag its ends to trim, drag empty space to scroll.

Keys: Space play/stop · M marker · L loop · S split at the cursor · C copy / X move the selection to the other track · Delete clip · 1 Select · 2 Move · − = zoom · Home 0:00 · ← → 1 s (⌥ 0.1 s) · ⌘Z undo · Esc clear.

Edits never change the original file: clips only point into it. Every clip edge gets a 3 ms fade so cuts don't click.
