Use in the score

Synth

Press and hold a pad, or hold its key on the keyboard (1–0, Q–Y); let go to hear the release. − / = octave, Space test phrase, Esc closes. Tap any number to type a value. Notes come from the score's field.

How to use the sketchpad

Drawing and selecting

Pencil stroke
draws a band (Band tool). Pressure = loudness, tilt = sine ↔ noise.
Quick Pencil tap
selects the unit under it instead of drawing.
Press and hold
on a unit with the Pencil picks it up; drag to move it.
⌘ + Pencil
selects in any tool; keep dragging to move. ⌘⌥ + drag moves a copy.
Finger tap
selects. Finger drag on the selected unit moves it. Fingers never draw.
Impulse
Pencil tap stamps a chord burst from the field (called Splash before; the Mac calls it Impulse too).
Block
drag a rectangle with the Pencil: the field lines inside it become a chord over that time (as on the Mac). Entry (chord, arpeggio, fan, strum) is in Settings.
Pulse
drag across time with the Pencil: a train of short notes at that pitch (end higher or lower for a glide). Gaps in ms, accelerando, length, jitter, accent and partials are in Settings (as on the Mac).
Line
draw with the Pencil: one voice that glides along the line (UPIC-style).
Cloud
drag a rectangle: a seeded scatter of notes between its edges. Density, note lengths, fade and a new seed are in Settings.
Layers
in Settings: tap a layer to draw on it (it is always shown in front). ● ◐ ○ = full · in the background · hidden. M = mute. Background all / Show all. Faded layers can't be selected.
✥ Select / move
switches the Pencil and trackpad from drawing to selecting and moving units; tap it again (or press S) to go back to drawing.
Settings: Unit | Score
Unit shows only the selected unit (or the brush for the next one); Score holds the global settings — sketch, field, duration, sound, layers. ⇧2 switches; selecting a unit or a drawing tool opens Unit.
Pulse bend
select a pulse (Select or Shape): drag its end points up or down, drag a ⊕ to add a bend point, double-tap (or ⌥-tap) a bend point to remove it. Points snap to the field; ⌘ = free pitch.
Zoom
two fingers on the score: spread / pinch = zoom in / out, move = scroll. Hold ⌥ to zoom pitch only, ⌘ to zoom time only. On the trackpad: ⌘ + two-finger scroll zooms time, ⌥ + two-finger scroll zooms pitch, plain two-finger scroll moves around (pinch also works where the browser lets it through). Keys: ] [ zoom time in / out, ⌥] ⌥[ zoom pitch, 0 = whole score — they zoom about the cursor (or the selected unit) and bring it to the middle of the screen. Or drag on the axes: the time ruler up / down = zoom time (then sideways = scroll); the pitch labels at either side up / down = scroll pitch, right / left = zoom pitch.
Notes on / off
select a block or an impulse: its notes show as chips in Settings › Unit. Tap one to switch it off (it stays as a faint ghost, silent) or on again. ⌥-click a note on the score does the same.
Chord (O)
drag across time to make a chord. While editing (Enter, or tap the chord with the Chord tool) tap in its column to add a note on the semitone grid, tap a note to remove it, drag a note to move it (⌥ = cents, ⌘ = free). Or type the notes in the panel: C3 E♭3 G3+14, MIDI 60.5, 440hz. The top / bottom handles stretch the spread (top keeps the bass, bottom keeps the top); dragging the chord up or down transposes by semitones (⌘ = free). Entry (chord, arps, fans, strum), velocity and envelope as for a block.
Start / end
in Select (or with ⌘, or a finger on a selected unit) drag a unit's left or right edge: the other end stays put and the content stretches to fit. With a group or several units selected, the whole selection stretches. ⌥-drag crops instead: the content stays in place (one unit).
Shape
drag the four corner handles; notes glide to follow the outline.
Line / cloud points
select a line or cloud (Shape or Select) to see its points: drag one to move it, drag a ⊕ to add one, double-tap (or ⌥-tap) to remove one. In Shape, drag a cloud's edge line to move the whole edge. Line points snap to the field; hold ⌘ for free pitch.
✎ Annotate
Write, Arrow, Circle, Note and Erase. Annotations sit on the score and make no sound.
Note icons
tap one to read or edit the note. Select a unit and press N (or Add note) to give it one.

Envelope panel (key 1)

Tap the curve
adds a point; keep dragging to place it.
Double-tap a point
deletes it (or tap it, then ⌫ / Delete point).
Tap between points
picks a segment; drag its ◇ up or down to bend it.
Draw freehand
starting away from the curve replaces that section.
● Record
plays the unit once while you ride the fader.
Reduce points…
slide down to thin the curve; the ends stay.
Shapes
Save shape… stores the envelope; tap a shape to load it.
Whole chord / a note
shape the unit, or give one note its own envelope.
Loudness · Pan
switch the lane between the loudness envelope and the unit's pan (top = right, middle = centre, bottom = left). Pan is drawn and edited the same way.
♪ Sound Lab (3)
in Settings: craft sounds from the REAPER synths (LH Breath so far). Hold the pads to play; moving a control on a REAPER preset makes your own copy. “Use on this layer” puts it in the score (Sound: Sine + Sound Lab presets).
LFO
adds a plain sine tremolo across the unit: shape, cycles, depth. More ▸ has the rest (Hz, phase, skews, tilt, baseline, soft edges, presets, Bake).
Section
then drag across the lane to put the LFO on part of the unit (⇧-drag with a keyboard). Drag the [ ] tabs at the foot of the lane to adjust it.

Playing

Space
play or stop from the teal start marker.
● Tap a rhythm…
select a pulse; in Settings tap the pad (or press T) in rhythm, then Done. Stretch the unit to compress or expand the rhythm.
▶ Unit · ⇧Space
play just the selected unit once. New units don't play by themselves, so you can edit them first.
Tap markers (⇧T)
tap a structural rhythm with P (or the Tap pad); each tap is a time point. The first tap is TP0 at the play point, then TP1, TP2… Press Space first to tap along with the score (taps land at the playhead). Return = done, Esc = cancel. Settings › Score has the button.
Regions / markers
the two slim lanes above the score. Tap the markers lane to add a marker (⇧M at the pointer); drag in the regions lane to make a region (⇧R from a time selection). Drag to move, drag a region's ends to resize, double-tap to name, ⌥-tap or ⌫ to delete. Saved with the sketch, listed in the Obsidian notes, and written to REAPER on Commit.
Time ruler
tap or drag it to move the start marker. , . nudge it, Return goes to 0:00.
Duration
in Settings. Changing it stretches or contracts the whole score (untick "Stretch" to keep material where it is).
L
loop (solo) the selected unit, or just the chosen note.
Silent switch
turn it off on the iPad to hear the preview.

Saving

Notes, writing, arrows and circles are saved in the sketch file, so they travel to the Mac with the score. Export notes for Obsidian (in Settings) saves them all as Markdown, each note with the date and time it was written, plus key data and a picture of the whole score.

Your work is kept on the iPad as you go, but Save writes the sketch file, which is the real copy. Save to iCloud Drive to reach the Mac. The red Unsaved dot shows when there are changes since the last save.
